# Build 12 — Retention & Readiness Intelligence

Build 12 activates the Practice shortcuts that previously looked available but were only informational placeholders.

## By Domain
- clickable
- choose Domains 1–4
- see current Active Practice evidence
- launch that domain's adaptive practice directly

## Weak Areas
- clickable
- ranks Needs Refresh, Learning, and weaker Usable concepts
- shows state, domain, attempts, and accuracy
- launches the appropriate domain's adaptive repair practice

## Retention & Readiness
Progress now summarizes:
- Stable concepts
- Developing concepts
- Needs Reinforcement concepts
- recent practice-exam average
- recurring Mixed Practice reasoning trap
- recommended next domain
- domain readiness signals
- evidence confidence: No evidence / Low / Moderate / High

The readiness view uses repeated in-app evidence and does not claim to predict the ISACA scaled score.
